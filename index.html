Minimalist Solderless 12V SLA & UPS Battery Charger

Overview: A 100% solderless, ultra-minimal, ~$3–5 12V SLA battery maintainer. No PCB, no regulator, no soldering iron. Just a $2 transformer, four diodes, and physics.

This open-hardware project is designed to keep 5Ah to 12Ah batteries topped up safely at a 13.8V float voltage, perfect for UPS and alarm systems.
